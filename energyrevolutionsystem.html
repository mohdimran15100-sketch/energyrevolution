Energy Revolution System: What It Is, What's Included & How It Works

Energy Revolution System is a digital DIY guide that teaches buyers how to build a small, supplemental electricity device using coil-winding concepts associated with Nikola Tesla's bifilar coil. It ships as a download, not as physical hardware.

Product format: digital guide, not a shipped kit

The clearest way to understand Energy Revolution System is by its delivery method: purchases are processed through ClickBank, and access is granted instantly through an online members' dashboard. There is no shipping step, no tracking number, and no box of solar panels or turbine parts arriving at your door. What you get is a set of downloadable PDF guides, diagrams and (per the seller's promotional cover art) several bonus e-books.

This is worth stating plainly because some promotional copy connected to this product in the past described "high-efficiency solar panels," "advanced wind turbines" and an "installation kit" you unbox — language that reads like a shipped renewable-energy kit. That description conflicts with the product's own checkout flow, its FAQ answers, its creator narrative, and its guarantee structure, all of which describe a hand-built, coil-based device assembled from commonly available parts. We treat the digital-guide description as authoritative and flag the solar/wind kit language as inconsistent legacy marketing that buyers should disregard.

What's included

Based on the product's own cover artwork and checkout page, a purchase includes:

  • The main Energy Revolution System guide, with the core build instructions and diagrams
  • A materials list describing the wire, coil form and basic circuit components needed
  • Several bonus e-books, covering related topics such as home-energy basics, battery storage, and alternative power sources
  • Mobile-friendly access to the guide through the members' dashboard

Bonus titles and exact contents are controlled by the seller and can be updated at any time. Treat the list above as a description of what was shown on the seller's own materials at the time of writing, and confirm current contents on the official checkout page before buying.

The technology concept: a bifilar coil, not photovoltaics

The build concept marketed for this guide centers on a bifilar coil — a coil wound from two parallel wires rather than one. This winding style is genuine, documented electrical engineering: Nikola Tesla patented a version of it in 1894 (U.S. Patent 512,340, "Coil for Electro-Magnets"), and bifilar winding techniques are still studied today in modern transformer and inductor design. See our dedicated explanation in the Tesla / Bifilar Coil section of the homepage.

Importantly, a documented 19th-century coil patent is not proof that any specific modern commercial guide "unlocks" free electricity. The physics of coils and induction is real; the leap from that physics to a specific product's marketed savings numbers is not independently verified.

How the build is presented to work

  1. Wind the bifilar coil following the guide's diagrams.
  2. Wire it into a small supporting circuit using common components.
  3. Test the assembled unit and troubleshoot using the manual.
  4. Use the finished project for small, supplemental loads.

This sequence reflects the seller's own description of the process. Neither this website nor any independent lab has published third-party verified performance data for this exact guide.

The science, briefly

Electromagnetic induction and bifilar coil winding are legitimate areas of electrical engineering, not pseudoscience. What is not established is the specific commercial performance of this guide's build — actual output is bound by the law of conservation of energy, meaning a home-built circuit cannot produce more usable electrical energy than the energy that goes into it. For the fuller science discussion, including cited sources, see the homepage science section.

How this compares to solar and wind installations

It helps to place Energy Revolution System next to the alternatives it's often compared with. A residential solar installation typically costs several thousand to tens of thousands of dollars, requires a professional installer, roof or ground-mount hardware, and can genuinely offset a meaningful share of a home's electricity use — supported by decades of DOE and NREL performance data. A small wind turbine setup carries similar installation costs and site requirements (consistent wind exposure, permitting, mounting). Energy Revolution System sits in a completely different category: a low-cost digital guide for a small, home-built circuit. It is not a substitute for solar or wind in terms of scale, and it should not be evaluated against the same savings expectations as a professionally installed renewable-energy system.

A closer look at the bonus materials

The seller's own bundle artwork shows the core guide packaged with several companion e-books — one focused on general home-energy concepts, one on battery storage, and one on alternative or unusual power sources. These read as supporting reference material rather than additional hardware products; think of them as background reading that expands on ideas mentioned in the main guide, not separate projects with their own parts lists. As with the main guide, exact bonus titles are controlled by the seller and may be updated over time.

Common misconceptions worth clearing up

  • "It generates free energy." No home-built circuit can output more usable electrical energy than the energy that goes into building and running it — that's the law of conservation of energy, and it applies here as everywhere else.
  • "Tesla proved this works." Tesla's 1894 patent documents a coil-winding technique. It says nothing about this or any other modern commercial product and does not constitute a scientific endorsement.
  • "It replaces solar panels." The guide markets itself as an alternative approach, but it is a small supplemental project, not a like-for-like replacement for a solar array's output.
  • "You get physical parts in the box." There is no box. Everything is a digital download, and buyers source their own materials locally.

Safety considerations

Any project involving wiring and electrical components carries risk if handled carelessly. Buyers should follow standard electrical-safety practices, work with low-voltage components as specified in the guide, avoid connecting any self-built device to household wiring or the utility grid, and consult a licensed electrician for anything involving mains voltage or permanent household wiring changes.

Limitations to keep in mind

  • Not designed or marketed as a replacement for your home's primary electrical service.
  • No independent third-party testing of the guide's specific savings claims is publicly available.
  • Results depend on build quality and the individual builder's skill.
  • Some legacy marketing copy about the product has been inconsistent, which buyers should factor into their expectations.
