How ClickBank affiliate marketing works, briefly

ClickBank is a digital-product marketplace that has operated since the late 1990s. Vendors list digital products (guides, courses, e-books) for sale, and independent affiliates — including review and informational sites — can promote those products using a unique tracking link. When someone buys through that link, the affiliate earns a commission and the buyer pays the same price they would have paid otherwise. This model explains why a single ClickBank product is often covered by dozens of independent websites, each with its own take, rather than by one single "official" publisher-controlled site the way a large retail brand might operate.

Red flags to watch for when searching

  • A site that claims to be the manufacturer but has no clear disclosure of an affiliate relationship.
  • Checkout pages that don't use recognizable, encrypted payment processing (look for HTTPS and a known processor like ClickBank).
  • Extreme urgency tactics — fake countdown timers or "only 2 left" messaging — layered on top of a digital product that has unlimited digital "stock."
  • Requests for payment through untraceable methods (wire transfer, gift cards, cryptocurrency to a personal wallet) instead of standard card/PayPal checkout.

To be clear: the presence of a ClickBank checkout and a 60-day guarantee are themselves reassuring signals for this specific product — the concerns above are general guidance for evaluating any similar offer you come across.
