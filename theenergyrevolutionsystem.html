The Energy Revolution System Explained: Concept, Contents & Who It's For

The Energy Revolution System is a digital, Tesla-inspired DIY guide for building a small supplemental electricity device. This page explains the concept plainly, in one place, for readers who just want the short version.

Product definition

Strip away the marketing language and the Energy Revolution System is, at its core, an instructional digital download. It teaches a method for winding and wiring a small coil-based circuit — grounded in the historical concept of Tesla's bifilar coil — that the seller presents as a way to generate a modest, supplemental amount of electricity at home. It is bought and delivered entirely online.

Product format

Digital only: a main PDF guide, a materials list, and bonus e-books, unlocked instantly in an online members' area after checkout. No hardware, panels, or turbines are shipped.

The technology, in one paragraph

A bifilar coil is wound from two parallel wires rather than one, a configuration Nikola Tesla documented in his 1894 patent for a "Coil for Electro-Magnets" (U.S. Patent 512,340). The tight coupling between the two wires changes how the coil's inductance and capacitance behave. That's genuine, citable electrical history. It is not, on its own, proof that a specific modern DIY guide meaningfully powers a household — see our full Tesla / bifilar coil explainer for the detailed, sourced version of this distinction.

The historical inspiration, in a bit more depth

Nikola Tesla was a prolific inventor who held hundreds of patents, many in electrical engineering and motor design. His 1894 patent, U.S. 512,340, titled "Coil for Electro-Magnets," described winding two parallel conductors together in a flat, pancake-style spiral. This bifilar arrangement changes the relationship between a coil's inductance (its resistance to changes in current) and its capacitance (its ability to store electrical charge), compared with a standard single-wire coil. Engineers still study bifilar winding today — for example, in peer-reviewed IEEE research on balancing transformers inside modern resonant power converters. That is a genuinely interesting piece of applied electrical history, and it is the honest, documented reason this category of DIY guide invokes Tesla's name. It is a separate question from whether any specific modern guide's finished build performs as advertised.

A general framework for evaluating DIY energy guides

Whether or not you buy this specific guide, the same checklist is useful for any similar digital product you come across:

  • Does the seller clearly state whether you receive a physical product or a digital download?
  • Are the science references (patents, research, agency data) checkable at a real, named source — not just asserted?
  • Are specific savings numbers backed by independent testing, or only by the seller's own marketing and testimonials?
  • Is there a real refund window, and through what payment processor?
  • Does the creator's identity and story hold up to a basic search, or is it unverifiable?

Applying this checklist to Energy Revolution System: it is clearly disclosed as digital once you reach the checkout, its Tesla/bifilar-coil reference is real and checkable, its savings figures are unverified marketing claims, its guarantee runs through the established ClickBank platform, and its named creator cannot be independently confirmed. That mixed picture — some genuinely solid elements, some unverifiable marketing — is typical for this category of product, and it's why a balanced, source-checked review matters more than either uncritical promotion or blanket dismissal.

Who it is for

This suits curious DIY hobbyists, budget-conscious learners, and anyone who wants a low-cost, hands-on introduction to coils and basic circuits. It is not designed for anyone expecting a certified, code-compliant replacement for their home's electrical service, and it is not a fit for buyers who aren't comfortable doing basic wiring work themselves.
